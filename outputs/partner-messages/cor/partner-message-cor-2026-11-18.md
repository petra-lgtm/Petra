Hi there,

I wanted to flag something on advertising this week, along with a simple change I'd like to make with your OK.

The new ads for the 15g Silver Soap bar ramped up faster than we wanted. Over November 9–15 they spent $105 and brought in 82 clicks, but no sales. The week before, November 2–8, spend was just $2. By November 14 and 15 they were running at around $21 a day, so we're trimming that back right away. We don't want ads on this product spending at that rate without results.

The bigger lesson is about which product we advertise. The 15g bar gets plenty of attention. It had 1,372 visits over November 9–15, mostly from shoppers finding it on their own. But fewer than 1 in 200 of those visitors bought. Your larger bars tell a completely different story. Across November 2–15, the $39 and $99 Silver Soap bars sold 31 units from just 59 visits, so roughly half of everyone who looked ended up buying.

That tells me shoppers who reach your full-size bars are ready to buy. The 15g bar, on the other hand, seems to draw curious browsers who aren't yet committed. Pointing our ad budget at the products that already convert should turn the same money into real sales, and it's a much stronger setup going into the holiday weeks.

So my one ask: are you happy for us to move the Silver Soap ad budget off the 15g bar and onto the full-size bars, starting at a modest $10 a day? We'll keep a close eye on it and report back on the first full week of results.

Thanks as always. It's lovely to see how well your full-size bars sell once shoppers find them, and I'd like to put more people in front of them.

Best,
