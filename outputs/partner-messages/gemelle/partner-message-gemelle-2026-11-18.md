Hi there,

I wanted to walk you through something we spotted in the ads this week, because there's a clear and fairly simple lever to pull before Black Friday.

Overall the store held steady. Sales over November 9–15 were $13,152, within 5% of the week before, and sales that came in without an ad behind them actually grew from $5,339 to $6,379. The ads, though, got less efficient: ad sales fell from $8,438 to $6,773 compared with November 2–8, and ACoS (ad spend as a share of ad sales) rose from 38% to 44%.

Almost all of that came from one place. Your main automatic campaign started showing much more on other products' pages and much less at the top of search results. On product pages, impressions jumped from about 107,000 to 435,000 in a week, but conversion fell from 17% to 7%, so ACoS there went from 23% to 44%. At the same time, the top-of-search placement, where nearly a third of clicks turn into orders, got about half the budget it had the week before ($478, down from $856).

In other words, the money is drifting to where browsers are, not where buyers are. Top of search was still delivering a healthy 32% ACoS on November 9–15, so it's simply getting too small a slice.

My one ask: are you happy for us to rebalance this campaign by raising the top-of-search bid adjustment and trimming bids on product-page placements, keeping total weekly spend about where it is now? We'd review the results with you after the first full week. If you'd rather we hold back until after Black Friday, just say the word.

Getting this right now means more of the holiday traffic lands in front of shoppers who are ready to buy, especially for the Gua Sha Oil, which carries most of the account.

Thanks so much, and I'm looking forward to a strong run into the holidays together.

Best,
Petra
