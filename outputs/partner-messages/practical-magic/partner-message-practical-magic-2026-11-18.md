Hi there,

I wanted to share a nice signal from last week, plus one easy opportunity I think we can grab before the holiday rush.

Over November 9–15, the store did $4,858 in sales from 179 units, up 35% on November 2–8. What I like most is how we got there. Visits to your listings were actually down about 15%, but far more of those shoppers bought. Across the store, about 5% of visits turned into a sale, up from 3% the week before.

The Midnight Margarita mixer was the standout. Its conversion nearly doubled, from 4% to 8%. The Sparkly Cocktail Mixers Variety Pack climbed from 5% to 6% and was again your top seller at $1,708 for the week. The Espresso Martini improved too, from 4% to 6%.

The opportunity is Moon Magic. It's getting almost as many visits as the Variety Pack (942 last week), but only about 2% of those shoppers buy, against 6–8% for its siblings at a similar price. People are clearly curious about it, so the gap looks like it's on the listing itself rather than on demand. Moon Magic's whole hook is that sparkly purple color, and I don't think the current images sell that moment as well as they could.

So my one ask: could you send us two or three photos of Moon Magic poured in a glass, ideally in good light where the purple shimmer really pops? Phone photos are perfectly fine. We'd use them to test a stronger lead image and gallery. Even lifting it to 4% would mean around 15 extra sales a week at today's traffic, and that's worth having in place before Black Friday.

It's lovely to see the core range converting this well. Thanks as always for being such a great partner to work with.

Best,
Petra
