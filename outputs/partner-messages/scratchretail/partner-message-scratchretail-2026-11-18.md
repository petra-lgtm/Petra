Hi there,

I've been digging into exactly what shoppers typed before they clicked your ads, and there's a nice, clear pattern worth acting on before the holiday rush.

Over the last two weeks, November 2–15, your best search by a distance was "wedding dress garment bag". It brought in $882 in sales from 14 orders on $145 of ad spend, an ACoS (ad spend as a share of ad sales) of just 16%. A cluster of more specific searches is also converting really well: "waterproof wedding dress garment bag", "garment bags for travel" and "long dress garment bags for travel" each turned roughly $5–10 of spend into $126 in sales. These are shoppers who know exactly what they want, and they're choosing you.

On the other side, some broader wedding searches are picking up clicks from people who aren't looking for a garment bag at all. "Wedding", "bridal", "wedding essentials", "destination wedding essentials" and similar terms took 62 clicks and $57 of spend over the same two weeks without a single order. That's money we'd much rather put behind the searches above.

So here's what we'd like to do. We'll give the proven, specific searches their own exact-match targeting so they get steady visibility and we control the bid on each, and we'll block the generic wedding terms so they stop drawing spend. The ads are already in good shape, with ACoS improving from 21% over November 2–8 to 19% over November 9–15, so this is about sharpening a strong setup rather than fixing a problem.

My one ask: are you happy for us to go ahead with those changes this week? If any of those broader wedding terms matter to you for brand reasons, just let me know and we'll keep them running on a lower bid instead.

Thanks so much, it's great to see the garment bag finding its audience so clearly.
