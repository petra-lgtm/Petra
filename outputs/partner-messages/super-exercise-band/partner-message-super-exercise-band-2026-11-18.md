Hi there,

I hope your week's going well. I've got a good-news number to share and one idea I'd love your view on.

Over November 9–15, sales came to $3,270, more than double the $1,500 from November 2–8. A big part of that was a single Amazon Business order for 28 of the 24-yard Sky Blue rolls, worth $848. Even without that order, the week was up about 60%. Business buyers made up 44% of your sales that week, which suggests professional and bulk buyers are finding you.

Here's where I think we can do better. Amazon shows us what share of the products seen on a given search are yours. For "resistance bands bulk", which gets around 340 searches a week, your listings made up roughly 1 in 120 of the products shoppers saw over November 1–7. Over November 8–14, that dropped to about 1 in 1,700. We saw a similar slide on "theraband roll", from about 1.5% to 0.6%.

These are exactly the shoppers looking for rolls, and bulk is where your bigger orders come from. The trouble is that paying for ads alone hasn't been efficient here. Over November 2–15 we spent $69 on ads for "resistance bands bulk" and got $86 back in sales. So rather than just raising bids, I'd like to give bulk buyers a better reason to choose you when they do find you.

So my one ask: would you be open to us setting up Amazon Business quantity discounts on the rolls? For example, a small per-roll discount at 5+ and 10+ rolls. If you're happy with the idea, could you let us know the lowest per-roll price you'd be comfortable with at each level? Amazon highlights these tiers to business buyers, and it gives us a stronger offer for the bulk searches we're currently missing.

Thanks so much. It's great to see larger orders like this coming through as we head toward the end of the year.
