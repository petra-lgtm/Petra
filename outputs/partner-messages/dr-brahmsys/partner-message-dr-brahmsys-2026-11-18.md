Hi there,

I've got a nice bit of progress to share on the advertising side, plus one quick decision that would help us push it further.

Over November 9–15, total store sales came in at $1,777, up 32% on November 2–8. Better still, the ads are working harder for that growth. Ad sales rose 21% to $1,158 while the cost of every click came down from $1.17 to $1.01, and ACoS (ad spend as a share of ad sales) improved from 65% to 62%. Sales that came in without an ad behind them grew even faster, up 59% to $619. That's exactly the pattern we want heading into the holidays: ads helping the Pet First Aid Kit build momentum that then carries on by itself.

The standout is the broad, low-bid campaign on the First Aid Kit. Over November 9–15 it spent $296 and brought in $734 in sales from 20 orders, an ACoS of 40% at just $0.85 a click. It's doing most of the heavy lifting on the account.

Two smaller campaigns are pulling the other way. One unnamed campaign, created on October 13, spent $67 for $30 in sales over the same week. The overnight Wander Flask auto campaign spent $25 and hasn't converted a sale yet. Together that's about $92 a week that could be working much harder.

So my one ask: are you happy for us to pause those two campaigns and move their budget into the low-bid First Aid Kit campaign? If the October 13 campaign is one your team set up for a specific reason, just let me know and we'll leave it running and look at bids instead.

With Black Friday and gift season ahead, getting more of every ad dollar onto what's already proven should lift sales and bring the overall ad cost down further.

Thanks so much, it's lovely to see the kit picking up steam like this.
