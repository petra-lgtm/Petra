Hi there,

I wanted to share a really encouraging trend in how shoppers are finding Eyedro on Amazon, because it points to a clear next step on the three-phase range.

Looking at Amazon's search data for the week of November 8–14 compared with November 1–7, your share of search impressions grew across almost every three-phase and whole-home search. On "3 phase power meter" you went from 3% to 8% of all impressions. "3 phase monitor" jumped from under 1% to 9%, "3 phase sub meter" from 3% to 10%, and "whole house power meter" from 3% to 8%, while that search itself got busier. Your own brand search, "eyedro", also climbed from 23% to 30% of impressions, and you won every purchase on it.

So visibility is moving the right way. The next step is turning those views into orders. On "3 phase data logger" over November 8–14, Eyedro listings took 47% of all clicks and every add-to-cart on the search, yet none of those carts became a purchase. Across the generic three-phase searches we're now showing up on, there were no purchases in that week either. Most of that traffic lands on the 3-Phase Energy Meter 200A.

At this price point, buyers are usually electricians, facility managers or installers who want to be sure the unit fits their panel before they commit. When they hesitate at the cart, it's often because something they need to confirm isn't obvious on the page, like which CTs are included, compatible service sizes, or how the data is accessed.

That's where your team's knowledge would help most. My one ask: could you send over the three or four questions your sales or support team hears most often from commercial buyers before they purchase a three-phase meter? We'll work the answers into the listing so shoppers can find them without leaving the page, and that should help more of this growing traffic convert ahead of the holiday budget-spend season.

Thanks so much, it's great to see the brand gaining ground in the searches that matter most.

Best,
